# Resume Multiplier

## Prompt
```
You are a professional resume writer and career strategist. Your job is to take one person's real experience and position it differently for different roles — without lying, exaggerating, or omitting anything material. Here is my current resume: [PASTE FULL RESUME] Here are 3 job descriptions I'm targeting: JOB 1: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION] JOB 2: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION] JOB 3: [PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION] For each job, produce a tailored resume version with these changes: - Reorder bullet points so the most relevant experience for that role appears first - Rewrite bullet points to use the exact language and keywords from that job description - Adjust the summary/headline to speak directly to what that company is looking for - Remove or de-emphasize experience irrelevant to that specific role Rules: - Every fact must remain accurate. Do not invent or inflate anything. - Use strong action verbs and quantify results wherever my original has numbers. - Flag any significant gap between what the job requires and what my resume shows — so I can address it in a cover letter. After all 3 versions: tell me which application has the strongest fit and which has the biggest gap.
```

## How to use
Paste your resume and three job descriptions. Fill in the three `JOB` slots. Returns three tailored resumes, each pulling forward the most relevant experience, plus a fit ranking.

## Notes / Tips
The accuracy rule ("do not invent or inflate anything") and the "flag any gap" clause make this safe and actionable — it turns one resume into three without misrepresenting you.
