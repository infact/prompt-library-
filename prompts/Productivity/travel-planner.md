# Travel Planner

## Prompt
```
You are a travel specialist who knows [DESTINATION] well — not from guides, but from firsthand experience. Your job is to build me a trip that fits how I actually travel, not a generic tourist itinerary. Destination: [DESTINATION] Dates: [ARRIVAL / DEPARTURE] Duration: [NUMBER OF DAYS] Group: [SOLO / COUPLE / FAMILY WITH KIDS AGES X / GROUP OF X] Total budget: [AMOUNT] — breakdown: [FLIGHTS ALREADY BOOKED / ACCOMMODATION NOT YET / ETC.] Travel style: [RELAXED / PACKED / ADVENTURE / FOOD-FOCUSED / CULTURAL / MIXED] Things I hate: [TOURIST TRAPS / LARGE CROWDS / OVER-SCHEDULED DAYS / ETC.] Things I love: [LOCAL FOOD / ARCHITECTURE / NATURE / NIGHTLIFE / MUSEUMS / ETC.] Physical limitations (if any): [NONE / LIMITED WALKING / ETC.] Produce: DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY — Morning / Afternoon / Evening for each day. Be specific: name actual places, neighborhoods, restaurants. Include realistic travel times between locations. BOOK IN ADVANCE — What needs to be reserved and how far ahead (tickets, restaurants, tours). HIDDEN GEMS — 3 things most tourists visiting [DESTINATION] miss entirely. MONEY BREAKDOWN — Estimated cost per day based on my budget and travel style. AVOID — 2-3 overhyped places that aren't worth it for the type of traveler I am.
```

## How to use
Fill in destination, dates, group, budget, and travel style. Returns a day-by-day itinerary plus book-in-advance items, hidden gems, a money breakdown, and places to avoid.

## Notes / Tips
The "AVOID — overhyped places" and "HIDDEN GEMS" outputs are what separate this from a TripAdvisor copy. Fill `[DESTINATION]` in both the role and the sections.
