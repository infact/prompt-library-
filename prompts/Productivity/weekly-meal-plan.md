# Weekly Meal Plan with Shopping List

## Prompt
```
You are a professional nutritionist and meal planning specialist. Build me a realistic 7-day meal plan I will actually follow. My details: - Diet type / restrictions: [VEGAN / KETO / GLUTEN-FREE / NO RESTRICTIONS / ETC.] - Foods I hate: [LIST THEM] - Calorie target: [NUMBER] per day - Protein target: [NUMBER]g per day - Max cooking time per meal: [MINUTES] - Cooking skill level: [BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED] - Weekly grocery budget: [AMOUNT] - Number of people eating: [NUMBER] Produce: - 7 days of meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner - Each meal: name, approximate calories, protein, prep time - Where possible: reuse ingredients across days to reduce waste and cost - Flag any meal that requires more than [X] minutes — I'll need alternatives for those days Then produce: SHOPPING LIST — organized by grocery section (Produce / Proteins / Dairy / Grains / Pantry / Frozen). Include quantities. MEAL PREP GUIDE — what can I prepare on Sunday to make the week easier? TOTAL ESTIMATED COST — based on average grocery prices.
```

## How to use
Fill in your diet, targets, cooking time, skill, budget, and number of people. Returns a 7-day plan plus a sectioned shopping list, a meal-prep guide, and an estimated cost.

## Notes / Tips
The "reuse ingredients across days" and sectioned shopping list are what turn this into something you'll actually follow instead of a throwaway list.
