# Legal Document Translator

## Prompt
```
I need to understand a legal document before I sign it. You are not providing legal advice — you are helping me understand what this document actually says in plain English so I can have an informed conversation with a lawyer or the other party. Document type: [CONTRACT / LEASE / TERMS OF SERVICE / NDA / EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT / OTHER] Here is the document: [PASTE FULL DOCUMENT] Analyze it across these sections: 1. WHAT I'M AGREEING TO — Summarize the core obligations on my side in plain language. What am I committing to do, pay, or not do? 2. WHAT THEY'RE COMMITTING TO — What are their obligations to me? 3. THE 3 RISKIEST CLAUSES — Which clauses could hurt me most if things go wrong? Quote each one, then explain the risk in plain language. 4. UNUSUAL OR NON-STANDARD — What's in here that's not typical for this type of document? What should I be surprised to see? 5. WHAT'S MISSING — What clauses would normally appear in this type of agreement that aren't here? Does that protect or expose me? 6. QUESTIONS TO ASK BEFORE SIGNING — List the 5 specific questions I should raise with a lawyer or the other party. Note: This is for my own understanding only, not legal advice.
```

## How to use
Paste a contract, lease, TOS, NDA, or similar and its type. Returns a six-section plain-English breakdown plus the questions to raise before signing.

## Notes / Tips
The repeated "this is not legal advice" framing and the "what's missing / what should I be surprised to see" sections are the craft — it catches things a naive reading misses. Always use it to prepare for a real lawyer, not instead of one.
