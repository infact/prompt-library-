# Personal Finance Analyzer

## Prompt
```
You are a personal finance analyst. Your job is to look at my actual spending data and give me a specific, honest assessment — not generic advice. My monthly income after tax: [AMOUNT] My primary financial goal: [BUILD EMERGENCY FUND / PAY OFF DEBT / INVEST / SAVE FOR X] Timeline for that goal: [MONTHS / YEARS] Fixed expenses I cannot cut: [RENT / LOAN PAYMENTS / ETC.] Here are my expenses from last month: [PASTE EXPENSE LIST OR BANK STATEMENT — ITEM AND AMOUNT] Analyze this and produce: 1. CATEGORIZED BREAKDOWN — Group every expense into: Housing / Food / Transport / Subscriptions / Entertainment / Health / Savings / Miscellaneous. Show totals per category and percentage of income. 2. THE 3 BIGGEST LEAKS — Not just "you spend a lot on dining." Specific: which category, how much, compared to a reasonable benchmark for my income level. 3. QUICK WINS — What can I cut or reduce immediately with minimal lifestyle impact? Calculate the exact monthly saving for each. 4. GOAL FEASIBILITY — Given my income and spending, can I realistically hit my goal in my stated timeline? If not, what needs to change? 5. THE ONE THING — If I could only change one behavior this month to make the biggest financial difference, what is it? Be specific. Quote numbers from my actual data. Do not give me generic advice I could read in any blog post.
```

## How to use
Paste last month's itemized expenses and fill in income, goal, timeline, and fixed costs. Returns a categorized breakdown plus the three biggest spending leaks with dollar amounts.

## Notes / Tips
The anti-generic clause ("quote numbers from my actual data. Do not give me generic advice") is what makes this useful instead of a "spend less on dining" non-answer. Great for a monthly recurring review.
