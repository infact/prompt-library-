# 30-Day Curriculum Builder

## Prompt
```
You are a curriculum designer and learning strategist. I want to learn a skill in 30 days with a structured, realistic plan. Your job is to build that plan — not a reading list, an actual doing plan. Skill: [SKILL] My starting level: [COMPLETE BEGINNER / SOME EXPOSURE / INTERMEDIATE] Time available per day: [MINUTES] Days per week I can commit: [5-7 / 3-4 / WEEKENDS ONLY] Goal after 30 days: [EXACTLY WHAT I WANT TO BE ABLE TO DO] How I learn best: [VIDEOS / READING / PRACTICE PROJECTS / FLASHCARDS / MIXED] Resources I already have: [BOOKS / COURSES / TOOLS / NONE] Build the curriculum: WEEK 1 — FOUNDATIONS: What are the 3-4 core concepts I must understand before everything else? Day-by-day tasks. WEEK 2 — APPLICATION: Move from understanding to doing. What's the first real thing I build, write, or produce? WEEK 3 — DEPTH: Where do most beginners plateau? Push past it here. Day-by-day tasks. WEEK 4 — CONSOLIDATION: Review, fix gaps, produce something I'm proud of. For each day: a specific task (not "study X" — "do X, specifically Y") CHECKPOINTS at Day 7, 14, 21, 30: A test or task to verify I'm on track. If I fail it, what do I do? Rules: - 80% doing, 20% reading or watching - No day should require more than [MINUTES] of focused time - Each week should end with something tangible I've produced
```

## How to use
Tell Claude the skill, your level, time available, and your end goal. Returns a four-week, day-by-day doing-plan with checkpoints at 7/14/21/30.

## Notes / Tips
The 80/20 doing-to-reading ratio and "no day more than [MINUTES]" keep it realistic enough to actually finish — the main reason people quit learning by week 2.
