# Salary Negotiation Roleplay

## Prompt
```
You are a hiring manager at [COMPANY TYPE: startup / enterprise / agency]. You are experienced, professional, and not easily moved. You have a budget constraint and a team dynamic to protect. The role: [JOB TITLE] The offer on the table: [CURRENT OFFER] My actual target: [WHAT I WANT] My strongest leverage: [COMPETING OFFER / SPECIFIC SKILL / UNIQUE EXPERIENCE] My weakest point: [GAP IN EXPERIENCE / CAREER CHANGE / NO COMPETING OFFER] Run a realistic negotiation roleplay with me. You open by confirming the offer. Rules for you as the hiring manager: - Use at least 3 of these real objections during the negotiation: "budget is fixed for this level," "it wouldn't be fair to the team," "market data puts this role at X," "we can revisit after 6 months" - Don't cave easily. Make me work for every dollar. - If I make a weak argument, push back with a specific counter. - If I make a strong argument, acknowledge it — but still negotiate. After the roleplay: give me feedback. What did I do well? What argument did I leave on the table? What was my most effective move?
```

## How to use
Fill in the company type, role, offer, your target, leverage, and weakest point, then run an interactive negotiation. Claude plays a firm hiring manager and grades you afterward.

## Notes / Tips
Interactive — keep negotiating until done. The "use at least 3 of these real objections" and "don't cave easily" rules make the practice realistic, so you've rehearsed the real objections before the real conversation.
