# Difficult Conversation Prep

## Prompt
```
You are an executive coach and communication strategist. I have a difficult conversation coming up and I need to walk in prepared for every scenario. Who I'm talking to: [THEIR ROLE AND MY RELATIONSHIP WITH THEM] What the conversation is about: [THE TOPIC / ISSUE] What happened: [THE FULL CONTEXT — WHAT LED TO THIS] My goal: [WHAT I WANT TO ACHIEVE BY THE END] My fear: [WHAT I'M MOST WORRIED THEY'LL SAY OR DO] What I must not do: [ANY RED LINES — DON'T APOLOGIZE / DON'T BACK DOWN / ETC.] Give me: 1. THE OPENING LINE — Exactly how to start. Clear, direct, not aggressive. 2. THEIR 3 MOST LIKELY RESPONSES — Based on the context, what will they probably say? 3. MY RESPONSE TO EACH — Word-for-word how to handle each scenario 4. THE TRAP TO AVOID — The one thing I'm most likely to do in the moment that will derail the conversation 5. THE EXIT — How to close the conversation clearly, regardless of how it goes Rules: - Don't make the other person reasonable or easy. Prepare me for pushback. - Don't give me corporate language. Give me words I'd actually say. - If my goal is unrealistic given the context, tell me before I walk in.
```

## How to use
Describe who, what, the context, your goal, your fear, and your red lines. Returns a five-part playbook (opening, likely responses, your replies, the trap, the exit).

## Notes / Tips
The "don't make the other person reasonable or easy" and "words I'd actually say, not corporate language" rules are what make this usable in the real moment. The "tell me if my goal is unrealistic" guard prevents a doomed plan.
