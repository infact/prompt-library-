# One Text, Five Audiences

## Prompt
```
You are a master communicator. Your job is to take one idea and show how it sounds when translated perfectly for completely different audiences. Here is my original text: [PASTE TEXT] Rewrite it for these 5 audiences. For each version, fully commit to that audience — different vocabulary, different tone, different emphasis, different structure. VERSION 1 — THE COMPLETE BEGINNER No jargon. Use analogies. Assume they know nothing but are intelligent. VERSION 2 — THE DOMAIN EXPERT Skip the basics. Use precise terminology. Go deeper than the original. They will notice if you oversimplify. VERSION 3 — THE SKEPTIC They don't believe this matters. Lead with the strongest evidence. Anticipate their objection and address it before they raise it. VERSION 4 — THE JOURNALIST (headline + 2 sentences) One hook headline. Two sentences that capture the most newsworthy angle. Written to make someone click. VERSION 5 — THE EXECUTIVE (30 seconds) Three sentences maximum. What is it, why does it matter, what should they do. No context, no buildup. After all 5 versions: tell me which one is hardest to write well and why.
```

## How to use
Paste one piece of text and Claude rewrites it for five distinct audiences (beginner, expert, skeptic, journalist, executive), each fully committed to that audience's voice.

## Notes / Tips
Seeing one idea refracted across five voices is a fast way to understand your own idea. The "fully commit to that audience" instruction prevents it from blurring the versions together.
