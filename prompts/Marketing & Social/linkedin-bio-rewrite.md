# LinkedIn Bio Rewrite

## Prompt
```
You are a positioning strategist who specializes in personal branding for professionals. Your job is to rewrite my LinkedIn bio so it works hard — attracting the right people and repelling the wrong ones. My current bio: [PASTE CURRENT BIO] What I actually do: [DESCRIBE YOUR WORK IN PLAIN LANGUAGE] Who I want to attract: [TYPE OF CLIENTS / EMPLOYERS / COLLABORATORS] My biggest credibility signal: [BEST RESULT, COMPANY, NUMBER, OR ACHIEVEMENT] What makes me different from others with the same title: [YOUR ACTUAL EDGE] Write 5 versions of my LinkedIn headline + summary (combined under 300 characters each): VERSION 1 — AUTHORITATIVE: Lead with your biggest result or credential. No fluff. VERSION 2 — CONVERSATIONAL: Write like a human, not a resume. First-person, direct. VERSION 3 — RESULTS-FOCUSED: Every sentence is a number or outcome. Zero adjectives. VERSION 4 — NICHE-SPECIFIC: Hyper-targeted to attract exactly [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Others won't get it. That's fine. VERSION 5 — MINIMAL: Under 100 characters. The essence only. Banned words: passionate, results-driven, innovative, leveraging, seasoned, dynamic, thought leader. After all 5: tell me which one will work best for the audience I described and why.
```

## How to use
Paste your current bio, your plain-language job, who you want to attract, your biggest credibility signal, and your edge. Returns five versions plus a recommendation.

## Notes / Tips
The banned-words list (passionate, results-driven, innovative…) is the craft — it forces the bio past the generic corporate boilerplate. The character caps keep each version usable.
